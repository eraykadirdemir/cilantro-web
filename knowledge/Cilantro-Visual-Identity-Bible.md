# Part I — Foundation

## Executive Summary
**✅ Verified Facts Influence:** Clean contemporary home-away-from-home, bright airy, hardwood fireplace, subtle cilantro green hints, family-owned Valentine's love story, best Mexican by lake, tableside guac theater, creative presentations.

**💡 Strategic Recommendation - Visual Summary:** The visual identity is **Minimal Modern Lake House Mexican** — not dark cantina, not sterile minimal. It balances Caregiver warmth (cream, wood, fireplace terracotta) + Creator creativity (black volcanic pot statement, berry margarita pop, cilantro leaf organic shape). Luxury level: Casual Premium $10-20 price feels like $$$$ care because everything bright clean and intentionally designed.

Core visual equity: (1) Cilantro Leaf Green as subtle sophisticated primary, not neon; (2) Volcanic Black as grounding for molcajete pot; (3) Warm Cream as airy background; (4) Terracotta as fireplace wood warmth; (5) Black Raspberry Berry as joyful accent. Typography clean geometric sans friendly, rounded 12-16px, soft shadows, natural materials.

## Visual Identity Strategy
**💡 Recommendation:** Own Top-Right quadrant: Contemporary Clean + Crafted Creative vs competitors Traditional + Generic. Strategy: Use restraint as luxury signal. Competitors use heavy red/yellow/green and Talavera overload. Cilantro uses white space, warm wood, subtle green, letting food color (pineapple yellow, berry purple, avocado green, molcajete black) be hero.

Three strategic levers:
1. **Cleanliness as Design:** White space = clean. Verified reviews praise bright clean classy. Design must feel sanitary premium like Sweetgreen, not cluttered.
2. **Theater as Identity:** Tableside guac grinding, molcajete sizzle, pineapple boat - motion and presentation become visual signature.
3. **Family as Texture:** Hardwood floors, linen, handwritten "Made with Love" accent, staff real photos - not corporate.

## Brand Visual Philosophy
**💡 Recommendation:** "We honor Mexican culture through flavor, hospitality, and subtle natural references — not through stereotypical decoration." Visual philosophy: Natural materials over decorative kitsch. Fresh ingredients are the decoration. Cilantro herb is inspiration not illustration overload.

## Brand Narrative Through Design
**✅ Verified Facts:** Mom Irma named restaurant and designed menu, opened Valentine's Day love goes in all they do, fireplace home away from home.

**💡 Recommendation Narrative:** Visuals tell story of Chesapeake family who moved to lake and built a bright home. Every visual choice should ask: Does this feel like Andrea invited you into her lake house living room (hardwood, fireplace, tasteful) and is cooking fresh for you in kitchen you can see (guac cart). If it feels like theme park Mexican, reject.

## Design Objectives
1. Make tiny strip mall exterior feel premium despite limited signage.
2. Make menu easily scannable with 24 sides + 8 enchilada styles + make-your-own.
3. Create Instagram-worthy still life for Pina Rellena/Molcajete that drives UGC.
4. Ensure Monday Closed prominent reduces frustration.
5. Build system scalable to catering packaging, merchandise, potential second location.

## Design Principles
1. **Bright & Airy First:** White/cream backgrounds dominate, dark only as accent.
2. **Subtle Green, Not Loud:** Cilantro green is whisper not shout.
3. **Rounded & Welcoming:** 12-16px radius, soft shadows, no sharp aggressive corners - friendly family.
4. **Material Honesty:** Show wood grain, stone molcajete texture, linen, avoid fake textures.
5. **Food as Hero:** Color comes from real dishes, not decoration.
6. **Tight-Shio Precision:** Alignment grid, consistent spacing 8px, staff-like tight ship service translates to tight visual system.

## Emotional Design Goals
Before: Curiosity + Trust (see clean bright 4.6 rating). During: Warmth (fireplace, wood) + Delight (pineapple boat surprise) + Comfort (familiar family). After: Pride (discovered best by lake) + Loyalty (tradition).

## Visual Positioning
**💡 Recommendation:** Position visually as **Contemporary Lake House Mexican - The Sweetgreen of SML** : Clean, ingredient-focused, bright, not heavy. Price $$ feels elevated through cleanliness and creative plating photography, not through white tablecloth.

## Luxury & Quality Perception Strategy
**✅ Verified Facts:** Reasonable price $10-20 great portions, but quality perception high per reviews "top notch", "best tasting dish I ever had".

**💡 Recommendation:** Achieve casual premium without pricing out: Use (1) Generous white space, (2) Soft diffused shadows vs harsh, (3) Real photography not stock, (4) Health Score 98 badge, (5) Material textures wood/stone, (6) Typography bold headings + readable body. Avoid gold foil excess (not brand). Quality signal = clean + fresh + tight.

## Contemporary vs Traditional Balance
70% Contemporary / 30% Traditional. Contemporary: layout, typography sans, bright minimal, rounded, no murals. Traditional: Molcajete stone pot, pineapple serving, family recipes, mother designed menu, cactus ingredient. Balance: We respect tradition in food soul, express it in contemporary home.

## Competitive Visual Analysis
**✅ Verified Facts:** Competitors near Moneta: Cancun 4.1, El Toreno 3.9, Mexico Viejo 2.8. Yelp snippet: Cilantro refreshing not tacky murals many favor.

**💡 Recommendation:** Competitors visual clichés: Dark interiors, sombrero fonts, excessive red/yellow/green, Talavera tile overuse, stock photos. Cilantro opportunity: Light, wood, fireplace, green accent limited, modern sans, real food photography with steam.

## Future Visual Evolution Strategy
Preserve core: green primary, cream background, black pot, bright clean, rounded friendly. Evolve: Refine leaf motif, add seasonal accent (berry for winter margarita, pineapple yellow for summer), introduce illustrations of 24 sides as icons, expand pattern for packaging. Never: Add sombrero, go dark cantina, chase heavy maximalist trend.

# Part II — Logo System

## Logo Strategy
**✅ Verified Facts:** No official logo files publicly available. Name Cilantro Mexican Grill with subtle green.

**💡 Recommendation:** Create interim wordmark system based on name equity. Avoid mascot. Equity is name "Cilantro" distinctive vs competitors "Cancun, Mexico Viejo". Strategy: Own Cilantro as distinctive. Make "Cilantro" primary, "Mexican Grill" secondary supportive.

## Brand Mark Philosophy
**💡 Recommendation:** Wordmark-led, not symbol-led. Symbol optional leaf/stone accent but not required. Philosophy: Family's name is brand. Like family signing - handwritten touch for love.

## Logo Construction Principles
**💡 Recommendation:**
- Font: Custom modified geometric sans (Poppins-based) for Cilantro, clean.
- C silhouette could integrate subtle leaf negative space but subtle not literal.
- Mexican Grill secondary in lighter weight smaller tracking.
- Keep readable at small sizes in strip mall signage from road.

## Logo Anatomy
**💡 Recommendation:**
- Primary lockup: CILANTRO (Bold 700) + Mexican Grill (Regular 400) beneath or to right with leaf dot over i? No, keep accessible.
- Optional Mark: Abstract cilantro leaf formed by three dots / stone molcajete circle.
- Spacing: Brand Bible x-height generous.

## Logo Geometry
**💡 Recommendation:**
- Construction on 8px grid. Cap height = x. "Cilantro" 100% cap, "Mexican Grill" 40% cap size if stacked, 55% if horizontal. Letter spacing -1% for Cilantro bold for tight family.

## Grid Construction
**💡 Recommendation:** Build on square grid: Cilantro word occupies 8 grid units wide, 2 units tall. Clear space = height of letter C. Grid helps consistency for signage.

## Clear Space Rules
**💡 Recommendation:** Minimum clear space around logo = 1x height of letter "C" in Cilantro on all sides. Never crowd with other logos or text. For small strip mall co-tenants, maintain.

## Minimum Sizes
**💡 Recommendation:** Digital: 32px wide for horizontal, 24px for icon. Print: 1" wide min horizontal. Favicon: 16px icon-only leaf.

## Maximum Sizes
No maximum but preserve proportions, don't stretch, ensure embroidery simplified version for aprons.

## Responsive Logo System
**💡 Recommendation:**
- Large Desktop Signage: Full horizontal lockup CILANTRO MEXICAN GRILL + possibly leaf mark.
- Tablet: Horizontal lockup smaller.
- Mobile/Nav: CILANTRO only wordmark (Mexican Grill drops under 768px if needed).
- Micro: Leaf icon only for favicon, social avatar.

## Horizontal Lockup
Primary: Symbol (optional leaf) Left, CILANTRO + Mexican Grill stacked to right. Use for website nav sticky, exterior signage long.

## Vertical Lockup
Stacked centered: Symbol top, CILANTRO middle, Mexican Grill bottom. Use for menus, business cards, window decals.

## Stacked Lockup
Same as vertical  but CILANTRO bold top, Mexican Grill small below, centered. Best for social avatar square.

## Wordmark System
**💡 Recommendation:** Cilantro wordmark alone usable when Mexican Grill understood via context (e.g., loyalty card, apron). Must keep same font weight.

## Symbol System
**💡 Recommendation:** Symbol optional: (1) Cilantro leaf abstract 3-lobed geometric, single green; (2) Molcajete stone circle top view abstract; (3) Combination leaf inside circle. Must work single color, not detailed illustration. Avoid too many lobes (hard embroidery).

## Icon-only Version
Leaf or molcajete circle icon for favicon, app icon, social avatar, packaging stamp.

## Monochrome Version
Single color versions: Black on light, white on dark, green #2E7D32 on light, cream on dark. Must maintain legibility.

## Reverse Version
White logo on charcoal #1A1A1A or green #2E7D32 or black raspberry #8E2F5B backgrounds for dark footer, bar menu.

## One Color Version
For emboss, stamps, single ink print.

## Embossed Usage
For business cards, loyalty cards, gift cards — blind deboss not gold unless anniversary. Keep subtle luxury.

## Engraved Usage
For wood signage interior, leather apron patch — simple line version.

## Foil Usage
Limited to anniversary Valentine's special only — gold foil on Valentine's menus. Not everyday.

## Favicon Strategy
**💡 Recommendation:** 16x16, 32x32, 180x180 Apple touch. Use leaf icon green on cream background rounded. Test on dark browser tab.

## App Icon Strategy
512x512, leaf icon centered on cream background with subtle shadow, rounded superellipse iOS. Should be recognizable at tiny size.

## Social Avatar Rules
Square 1080x1080, stacked lockup centered on cream, safe area 20% padding. No text too small - if needed, CILANTRO only not full. Consistent across FB, Instagram, Google Business.

## Safe Area Rules
As clear space rules. No other element enters 1x C height.

## Logo Placement
**💡 Recommendation:** Website top-left. Exterior signage: above door centered, but ensure visible from road Mercantile St. Interior: Above fireplace tastefully small not overwhelming. Menus top-center. Packaging top-left. Uniform left chest.

## Incorrect Logo Usage
Never: Stretch, rotate, drop shadow heavy, outline stroke, change colors outside palette (no red primary), add sombrero, use tacky font, place on busy photo without contrast box, use low-res Yelp photo, use black on black.

## Logo Animation Principles
**💡 Recommendation:** Subtle: Fade in, leaf gentle float 6s loop, or guac grind micro motion for digital loading. Not spinning, not bouncy.

# Part IV — Typography

## Typography Philosophy
**💡 Recommendation:** Typography should feel like tight-ship service - precise, readable, friendly, not decorative. Clean modern lake house invitation.

## Font Selection Strategy
**💡 Recommendation:** Choose widely available Google Fonts for easy implementation without license issues, but premium feel. Pair geometric sans heading (friendly bold) + humanist sans body (readable). Avoid decorative Mexican-style fonts. Avoid serif heavy traditional.

## Primary Typeface
**Poppins (Recommendation)** - Geometric sans, friendly rounded terminals, modern, 7 weights.
Why: Rounded friendly matches rounded corners 12-16px, welcoming. Bold 700 for CILANTRO stands out road signage. Good readability menu.
Weights: 400 Regular for body, 500 Medium for subheads, 600 Semibold for cards, 700 Bold for hero.
Alternative if license: Montserrat.

## Secondary Typeface
**Inter (Recommendation)** - Humanist sans highly legible at small sizes, for body, menu descriptions, paragraphs.
Why: Optimized for screens, readable for 24 sides list, menu descriptions, hours.
Weights: 400, 500, 600.
Alternative: DM Sans.

## Fallback Fonts
Primary fallback: system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif.
Monospace fallback: ui-monospace, SFMono.

## Display Typography
Display for special hero: Poppins Bold 72px desktop / 48px mobile, line height 1.05, letter spacing -2%, used for "Contemporary. Classic. Delicious." or "Made with Family Love".

## Heading Scale
Modular scale 1.25:
- H1: 48px desktop / 36px mobile - Poppins Bold - line-height 1.1
- H2: 36/28 - Poppins Semibold 600
- H3: 28/22 - Poppins Semibold
- H4: 20/18 - Poppins Medium 500
- H5: 16/14 - Inter Semibold uppercase tracking 5%
- Menu Dish Name: 18px Poppins Semibold

## Body Typography
Body: Inter 16px desktop / 15px mobile, line-height 1.6, color charcoal #1A1A1A on cream, paragraph spacing 1em, max width 65ch.
Menu Description: Inter 14px, line-height 1.5, color 80% charcoal.
Caption: 12px Inter.

## Caption Typography
Caption: Inter 12px 400, uppercase maybe for categories "Appetizers" tracking 8%, color medium gray.

## Numerical Typography
Prices: Use Poppins Medium 16px, tabular numbers for alignment in menu - font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums. Price not dominant - dish name dominant.

## Monospace Usage
Only for order codes, coordinates maybe. Use Inter mono or SFMono.

## Responsive Typography
Mobile: Scale down 15%, line height increase slightly 1.65 for readability. Headings reduce more. Never below 14px body for accessibility.

## Line Heights
Headings 1.1-1.2 tight friendly, body 1.6 spacious airy (like interior), caption 1.4.

## Letter Spacing
Headings Bold -1% to -2% tight premium, body 0%, captions uppercase +8% tracking.

## Paragraph Width
Max 65-75 characters for readability, center for story.

## Reading Comfort
Dark charcoal on cream not pure black on white reduces eye strain, line height 1.6, paragraph spacing, chunk menu categories with white space.

## Accessibility Typography
Minimum 16px body, contrast 16.5:1, line height min 1.5, focus visible, not rely on color alone for dish badges.

## Typographic Hierarchy
H1 Hero > H2 Section > H3 Dish Category > H4 Dish Name > Body Description > Caption Meta. Use size, weight, color to hierarchy, not many fonts.

# Part V — Iconography

## Icon Philosophy
**💡 Recommendation:** Line icons with rounded caps, friendly not sharp, handmade slightly imperfect not mechanical, ingredient-inspired.

## Icon Grid
24x24 base grid, 2px stroke, 2px padding safe area. Design on 24 grid, scale to 16,20,32,48.

## Stroke Width
2px default, 1.5px for 16px small, 2.5px for 48px large - constant visual weight.

## Corner Radius
2px rounded caps and joins - friendly consistent with radius system.

## Filled Icons
Use filled for active states: e.g., heart filled for favorite California Burrito, location pin filled active.

## Outline Icons
Default outline for nav, menu categories, features (patio, bar, parking, wheelchair, pets, kids).

## Animated Icons
Subtle: leaf sway 2s ease-in-out for loading, molcajete steam rising 3s loop, guac bowl pulse.

## Icon Sizes
16px inline microcopy, 20px menu, 24px default UI, 32px feature cards, 48px hero.

## Icon Accessibility
Always with text label, aria-hidden if decorative, title for standalone, contrast meets AA.

## Icon Usage Rules
Custom set: cilantro leaf, avocado, pineapple (Pina Rellena), molcajete pot, margarita glass, fireplace, patio umbrella, bar, clock (hours), parking, wheelchair accessible, pets paw, kids (use Not publicly verified icon set but simple), 24 sides icon (custom). Avoid sombrero, chili pepper overuse.

# Part XIII — Motion Identity

## Motion Philosophy
**💡 Recommendation:** Motion like service: Quick courteous, not slow sluggish, not hyper. Human timing.

## Motion Personality
Warm, quick, precise, slight bounce friendly but professional tight-ship.

## Timing
- Micro interactions: 150-200ms
- UI transitions: 250ms
- Page section fade up: 400ms
- Hero: 600ms

## Duration
- Fast: 150ms hover
- Base: 250ms
- Slow: 400ms emphasis
- Never >600ms.

## Easing Curves
- Default out: cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1) - ease-out-expo soft landing
- In-out: cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)
- Emphasized: cubic-bezier(0.34,1.56,0.64,1) slight overshoot friendly for badges
- Linear for loading.

## Hover Motion
- Buttons: lift 2px + shadow md -> lg + background darken 8% 200ms out expo
- Cards: lift 4px + shadow lg 250ms
- Images: scale 1.03 subtle zoom inside frame overflow hidden 400ms

## Scroll Motion
Fade up + 16px translate Y, stagger 80ms between cards, threshold 20% viewport. Respect reduced motion.

## Loading Motion
- Skeleton cream with green shimmer leaf pattern
- Molcajete steam rising loop 3s for menu loading
- Cilantro leaf rotate 1.5s linear infinite for small loader.

## Page Transitions
Fade + slight slide 16px up, 300ms.

## Micro Interactions
- Heart favorite burrito: scale pop 120% 200ms
- Guac add-on toggle: switch with leaf icon
- Monday Closed badge pulse subtle 3s.

## Reduced Motion Accessibility
@media (prefers-reduced-motion) disable scroll motion, keep fade only, no parallax.
