# Brand Story

**✅ Verified Facts Only - No Invention:**

Cilantro Mexican Grill opened February 14, 2023 — Valentine's Day. It's a fitting day to open for a place where you can tell love goes in all they do.

Owners Jose and Irma Perez and their daughter Andrea are from Chesapeake. They moved to the lake five years ago with her two sisters. Andrea started out helping in the family business at a young age, and worked way up to becoming one of the owners and a manager, alongside her mom and dad. That makes it feel like home away from home. That's exactly what owners and staff strive for — making each guest feel like family as they indulge in modern Mexican cuisine. It's contemporary. It's classic. In a word, it's delicious.

The menu brings a new twist on Mexican food with dynamic mix of authentic and modern dishes. Fresh guacamole made tableside. Warm homemade chips. A signature Black Raspberry Margarita — simple, refreshing, beautifully balanced. A beautiful Pina Rellena served in half pineapple. And one signature dish you will want to try — the Molcajete, served in large black pot overflowing with grilled chicken, ribeye steak, shrimp, pork chop, sausage, cactus and onions, with two jalapeno stuffed peppers, rice, beans, sour cream, salad, tortillas on side — a conversational piece that tastes as good as it looks.

In its short time, it's quickly become the best Mexican restaurant by the lake according to locals and visitors who keep coming back, driving 40 miles, making it their first stop each return trip to Smith Mountain Lake.

# Tone of Voice

**✅ Verified Facts on Current Tone:** Facebook post casual friendly inclusive: "We will be closed this upcoming week for our little summer break June 29th - July 6th! Come see us before we close and if not we will see you guys on the 7th! Happy 4th of July!"

**💡 Strategic Recommendations:**

- **Personality:** Warm Family Host. Not corporate, not overly formal, not slangy. Like Andrea speaking to guest you know by name.

- **Vocabulary:** Simple, fresh, family, love, home, bright, clean, contemporary, welcoming. Use food verbs: sizzling, fresh-made, warm, piping hot, overflowing, generous. Avoid: authentic in cliché way, fiesta/ole sombrero stereotypes, "exotic".
