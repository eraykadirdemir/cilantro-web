✅ *Verified Camera Angles:* 0–45° and 90° are go-to for food photography. Consistently using yields professional composition.

## Composition Rules

Rule thirds, leading lines, framing, fill-the-frame. Arrange elements viewer eye lands key subject.

✅ *Verified:* Fill frame means using entire frame so no part looks empty unrelated. Place primary elements dish/person in thirds to guide eye. Leading lines tables rails lines tortilla chips converge toward subject.

💡 *Recommendation:* Avoid splitting frame awkwardly. If using architecture/table edges lines ensure lead into scene. Use foreground objects utensil/garnish for depth. For hero include foreground chips basket frame background Molcajete.

**Framing:**
- Tight crops dishes fill most frame minor bleeding OK little negative space breathe.
- Contextual frames interior include architectural elements door frames windows natural frames fireplace mantel.
- Portrait frames people slightly wider headroom top eyes upper third.

**Depth of Field:**
Shallow DOF wide aperture isolate subjects e.g. one drink/person blurs background creamy bokeh pop. Deeper DOF narrow aperture everything focus e.g. overhead group shot.

✅ *Verified:* Shallow DOF compositional choice help viewer move to most important part frame.

## Lighting Philosophy

Light mood-maker. Favor soft natural or warm artificial avoiding harsh contrasts. Goal enhance textures colors without overpowering. Ratio highlights/shadows moderate maintain detail.

✅ *Verified:* Soft light reveals form detail without harsh shadows. Emulate diffused daylight: window light or softbox diffusion. For every source use reflectors bounce boards gently fill shadows.

**Natural Lighting:** Prioritized whenever possible authentic atmosphere. Interior day disable interior lights rely window light balanced reflectors. Outdoor golden hour or soft overcast. Shoot near large windows facing north even daylight or east/west morning/evening warmth. Color temp around 5500K daylight. White/black cards reflectors adjust fill.

**Artificial Lighting:** When needed night/low-light use warm-tone 3000–4000K LED/tungsten to match cozy vibe. Place high/angled mimic sunlight avoid flat frontal flash. Softboxes umbrella diffusers bounced flash soft.

**Golden Hour Strategy:** Golden hour ~1 hour after sunrise before sunset signature magical warm side lighting long shadows. Plan hero outdoor shots window. Use photograph patio guests or close-ups fajitas sizzling near window. HerVerified: warm light evokes intimacy richness. Note local sunrise/sunset schedule.

**Color Temperature:**
- Daylight neutral/slightly warm 5000–6000K
- Evening interior warm 3000–3500K cozy
- Avoid mixing half daylight blue half tungsten orange simultaneously balance/correct post
- Cocktails/produce maybe cool slightly enhance colors margarita more vibrant ~5600K

**Shadows/Highlights:** Shadows soft-edged reveal form not harsh silhouettes avoid distracting dark areas ensure important details faces hands steam fall in light. Highlights gentle specular glints on water glass metal not blown-out clip minimal hot spots slightly underexpose preserve detail brighten post.

- [ ] Matches color palette #2E7D32 #FFFBF2 #D87C45 #1A1A1A #8E2F5B and lighting warm natural fireplace glow
- [ ] Composition aspect ratio correct for intended use 16:9 hero 4:5 social 1:1 feed
- [ ] Props food items authentic match verified menu: California Burrito contains shrimp chicken steak avocado, Pina Rellena contains pineapple boat, Molcajete contains cactus etc - no invented ingredients
- [ ] Emotion atmosphere align family warmth caregiver primary archetype not lonely individual
- [ ] No forbidden styles sombrero dark HDR
- [ ] Bright clean light airy interior shows hardwood floors fireplace tasteful no tacky murals
- [ ] Monday Closed rule not violated in hours visuals if shows

This ensures any new asset feels like Cilantro.

## Seasonal Art Direction

Reflect seasonal changes subtly. Summer campaign lush green foliage bright sunlight backgrounds; spring blossoming flowers pastel tableware. Fall/winter autumnal tones oranges browns layering woolen throw chair hearty soups Birria stew. Holiday-themed visuals Dia de los Muertos not year-round style reserve any holiday imagery relevant seasonal promotions even then tasteful gently lit candle table.

- **Fall/Winter:** Deep reds oranges can appear salads garnishes alongside terra tones plaid textiles darker wood warmth add small candles rich soups. Fireplace more prominent.
- **Spring/Summer:** Brighter greens yellows fresh lettuce citrus prevail fresh flowers table decor lighter wood increased daylight shots. Patio outdoor seating hero.
- Use seasonal produce dishes squash fall naturally introduce seasonal color palettes.

## Do's & Don'ts

**Do:**
Use authentic fresh ingredients real people. Maintain warm consistent lighting natural window + fireplace glow. Follow compositions rule thirds fill frame leading lines. Create emotional narrative-rich images anticipation discovery enjoyment contentment. Use brand colors props consistently terracotta plates black volcanic pot wooden tables. Include contextual cues plates table settings cilantro plant fireplace that tie back to identity. Show generous portions huge margaritas no shortcuts. Show hardwood floors tasteful decorations no tacky murals. Show near Moneta Library tiny strip mall but worth it.

**Don't:**
Use cliché Mexican imagery sombreros cartoon skulls chili pepper overload desert scenes generic tacos. Don’t use flashy filters unnatural color tints blue cold. Avoid mismatched lighting color across images day half blue tungsten orange. Don’t overcrowd shot leave breathing space white space = clean. Never contradict brand voice images lonely individuals conflict family focus. Don't invent menu items pricing awards history not verified. Don't show dark cantina heavy shadows. Don't use HDR glitch prism flares. Don't place floating ingredients without context.
